Sushi

Sushi consists of seasoned Japanese rice combined with fresh fish, vegetables, and other fillings and wrapped or shaped into elegant bite-sized pieces.

1 300 g sushi rice
2 4 sheets nori (dried seaweed)
3 1 tablespoon sugar
4 150 g sushi-grade salmon
5 150 g sushi-grade tuna
6 1 tablespoon sesame seeds
7 1 cucumber
1
Rinse the sushi rice several times under cold water until the water becomes mostly clear.
2
Cook the rice with the measured water according to the rice cooker's instructions.
3
Mix the rice vinegar, sugar, and salt until dissolved.
4
Transfer the cooked rice to a wide bowl and gently fold in the vinegar mixture while the rice is warm
5
Allow the seasoned rice to cool to room temperature.
6
Slice the salmon, tuna, cucumber, and avocado into thin, even strips.
7
Place a sheet of nori on a bamboo sushi mat and spread a thin, even layer of rice over it.
